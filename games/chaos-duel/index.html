The basics

  • Best of three: both fighters start each round with 100 HP. Win two rounds to win the match. When the timer runs out, the healthier fighter takes the round.
  • Special button: press it alone to use your special ability when its icon glows. Press it while holding left or right to dash.
  • Combos: a punch that connects can chain into up to three punches. The third one sends your opponent flying.
  • Blocking stops most damage but drains your guard bar (under your health). If it empties, your guard breaks and you're stunned.
  • Heavy attacks knock down. A fighter getting up is briefly invulnerable, so no cheap pile-ons.
