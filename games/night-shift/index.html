HOW TO PLAY

You wake inside St. Aldric Memorial Hospital. The power is almost gone and the patients are no longer patients. Find keys, unlock your way through four chapters and escape.

W A S D Move
Mouse Look
Shift Sprint (makes noise)
Space Jump
E Interact / pick up
F Flashlight
Left click Shoot
R Reload
H Use medkit
Tab Inventory & notes
Esc Pause
  • Walkers shamble slowly. Runners lie dormant until you get close or make noise — then they sprint. L-09… don't let it reach you.
  • The infected hunt by sound and light. Gunshots and sprinting carry far. A lit flashlight is seen from further away.
  • Headshots kill instantly. Ammo is scarce.
  • Your progress is saved at the start of each chapter. Use CONTINUE to return.
